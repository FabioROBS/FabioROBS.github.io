I am a Master's student in **Mechatronics and Robotics Engineering** at **Politecnico di Milano**, passionate about robotics, research, and innovation.

My primary interests lie in **autonomous mobile robotics**, **multi-robot and multi-agent systems**, and **legged and humanoid robotics**. I am fascinated by how decentralized robotic teams, swarm intelligence, and autonomous mobile platforms can perceive, navigate, and collaborate to solve complex challenges in unstructured, dynamic environments.

I am particularly enthusiastic about the full robotics stack: from low-level automation, state estimation, and optimal control (MPC) to high-level perception, path planning, and learning-based control using deep learning. What drives me most is bridging rigorous theory with hands-on robotic development—turning mathematical algorithms into reliable, real-world robotic systems.

### Research & Project Highlights

* **Mobile Manipulation & Agriculture:** As a Research Assistant Intern at **JOiiNT LAB - Italian Institute of Technology (IIT) / Robotic Intelligence League Bergamo**, I developed navigation improvements, path planning, and obstacle avoidance for a mobile manipulator in agricultural robotics.
* **Humanoid Robotics:** As a Software Developer Intern at **Oversonic Robotics**, I developed software applications and Gazebo simulations for a multitasking humanoid mobile robot, focusing on robot versatility and real-world task execution (such as autonomous door opening).
* **Quadruped & Legged Robotics:** As software team leader in **AEA (Polimi Robotic Association)**, I led development on a micro version of Boston Dynamics' SPOT quadruped, training actuator neural networks using ROS 2, Gazebo, and Python to enhance movement and locomotion control.
* **Medical Robotics:** During my exchange semester at **Technische Universität Hamburg (TUHH)**, I worked with the **mtec lab** developing a robotic biopsy system using a Franka Emika Panda manipulator with ROS 2, OpenCV, and Open3D.
